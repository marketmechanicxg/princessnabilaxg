Things I've
never said aloud

To you, always —

I want you to know that I noticed you before you ever noticed me. I watched you exist in a room once and thought: oh, so this is what it feels like. Like recognizing something you've never actually seen.

You laugh at things other people miss. You make space for people without making noise about it. You are soft in the exact places the world usually makes people hard. That's rare. That's not small.

There are days I want to rewind to the first time we talked, just to feel that second again — the one where everything shifted, where some quiet version of me understood this person is going to matter.

I don't know what this is, exactly. But I know it's the most honest thing I've ever built. And I'd build it again. Every time. In every universe. Always back to you.

— yours, completely.
